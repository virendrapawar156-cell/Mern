Breaking Bad is a critically acclaimed American crime drama series that aired on AMC from 2008 to 2013. Set in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the story follows Walter White (Bryan Cranston), a mild-mannered high school chemistry teacher who receives a terminal lung cancer diagnosis. Desperate to secure his family's financial future before he dies, Walter partners with a former student, Jesse Pinkman (Aaron Paul), to manufacture and distribute pure blue crystal methamphetamine. Over five gripping seasons and 62 episodes, the series charts Walter's dark transformation from a desperate family man into a ruthless drug kingpin known as "Heisenberg", navigating high-stakes conflicts with drug cartels, local kingpins, and his own DEA agent brother-in-law, Hank Schrader.

Walter White

Walter White is the protagonist of Breaking Bad, a high school chemistry teacher who transforms into a ruthless drug kingpin known as "Heisenberg".

Jesse Pinkman

Jesse Pinkman is a former student of Walter White and his partner in the methamphetamine business.

Hank Schrader

Hank Schrader is a DEA agent and Walter White's brother-in-law and operator.
